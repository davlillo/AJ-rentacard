# Unified workshop cost entry and collection focus
Objective: One workshop cost entry that asks how much was paid, and a collections section that leads with what the customer still owes.
Branch: feature/pruebas; local only, no commits/push, no database writes or migrations. Checks: isolated tests, synthetic browser fixtures, TypeScript and production build. Engram mirror pending unresolved project identity.

## Specs
- S1: “deja la opcion de que si comprarn para pagar despues” — Keep credit purchases first class: a cost may be acquired and left pending with the supplier. The new entry must offer that state explicitly, not as an accident of missing fields.
- S2: “pero tambien ellos mas que todos es cosas pendientes por cobrar mas que todo” — In the workshop collections section the customer receivable leads: pending balance first, then the primary collection action, with the supplier credit shown as a separate fact so the two are never confused.
- S3: One obvious entry replaces the three mechanism-labeled buttons. It asks “¿Cuánto pagaste?” and routes to the canonical commands only: paid in full records the cash outflow and the cost share together; paid in part acquires the cost and records a first supplier payment; paid nothing acquires the cost and leaves the supplier payment pending for the existing later action.
- S4: “Registrar costo” must remain permission gated and must never create a customer charge, a receipt of income or an automatic client price. Manual client pricing stays in the quote workflow; the cost entry only records what the shop acquired and paid.
- S5: Partial failure must be recoverable: when the cost is saved and the first supplier payment fails, the cost stays, the pending supplier amount is exact, and only the payment is retried with the same identity. No duplicate cost, no duplicate cash, no silent loss.
- S6: Money semantics stay canonical: a supplier payment is a cash outflow, a customer collection is an incoming amount, an applied advance is not new cash, and an issued receivable is not a forecast. The workshop scope balance keeps coming from the server.
- S7: Preserve every existing capability and permission: linking an already registered expense stays available as a secondary action, the later “Registrar pago al proveedor” action stays, the quote approval and cost disposition flow stay, versions and idempotency keys stay, and the current permission plus case access gate keeps hiding protected amounts. No schema change, no new accounting rule, no stored artifact.
- S8: Verify with isolated tests and synthetic browser fixtures at four widths, then the full suite and the production build. Keep the shared collection form default behaviour for rentals unchanged.
