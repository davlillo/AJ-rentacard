# Workshop logic, labor/material split and stage flow
Objective: Make the workshop flow logical and finished: labor separated from material, additional expenses visible where they belong, tasks present but never mandatory, no bold disclosure arrows, and the folio advancing to the next stage by itself.
Branch: feature/pruebas; local only, no commits/push until the parent verifies. Checks: focused node tests, synthetic browser fixtures, TypeScript, full suite and production build at the end.

## Specs
- S1: “Separar mano de obra y material (repuestos)” — The budget and the folio must present labor separately from parts. The data already carries a line kind (Material, Trabajo, Tercero, Indirecto); use it. Show subtotals per kind where the money is read, keep one client total, and never invent a second total that contradicts the sum of the lines.
- S2: “Gastos adicionales … si yo agrego un gasto adicional … no lo vi reflejado” — An additional expense must be visible where the operator looks: after classifying a cost as an additional item (or adding an extra budget line), the folio must show it plainly with its amount and its state (pending approval, approved, invoiced), and it must be part of the agreed amount once approved. No orphan amounts.
- S3: “no podía cerrar algo de taller porque me decía algo de las tareas abiertas … yo te pedí que dejaras tareas pero con una mejor UI y que no sea obligación” — Open tasks must NOT block delivery. They stay visible, with a clear place and a plain note that they are optional and can be closed later. Pending costs and an undecided budget revision keep their checks, because those are money.
- S4: “unas flechitas negritas que es para ver campos, eso no me gusta, siento que en UI eso no termina el sistema” — Replace the raw `<details>/<summary>` disclosures in the folio surfaces with a finished, styled disclosure (a proper button row with a chevron icon, hover and focus states, and an accessible expanded state). Content that is short and always relevant is shown directly instead of hidden.
- S5: “uno no se va automáticamente a las etapas, hay que darle clic a la burbuja roja … ese seguimiento no está bien” — After a successful action, the folio must advance by itself to the stage that now applies, without the operator hunting for the next circle. The circles stay as navigation, but they stop being the only way to move.
- S6: Preserve every command, payload, permission, guard, version rule, idempotency key and money meaning. No new accounting rule, no silent issuance, no removal of a server check that protects money.
